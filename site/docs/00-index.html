Run goose and you get a full-screen app in the style of OpenCode: search ~1,650 artists, open a discography, press p. Music plays through mpv in the background, so it keeps going after you close the window, and every other terminal (or script, or keybinding) controls the same player.

Thirty-second tour

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lubabs770/gooseneck/main/install.sh | sh
goose doctor                 # check mpv, yt-dlp and friends
goose                        # open the TUI
goose play "8th Day" lucky   # …or skip it entirely
goose radio fried            # endless radio from an artist

What's in the box

  • A catalog: artists.json, ~1,650 artists tagged Chasidish, Israeli, A cappella, Kids and more, refreshed daily.
  • A TUI: browse, queue, play, download, change themes, all from the keyboard.
  • A CLI: every TUI action as a goose command, with --json output and shell completion.
  • A small daemon that scrobbles your listens and keeps radio going, even with the TUI closed.
